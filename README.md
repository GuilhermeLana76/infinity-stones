# 💎 Infinity Stones — An Interactive MCU Experience

An interactive web experience inspired by the **Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)**, exploring the six Infinity Stones and their powers, history, and significance throughout the universe.

The project combines **front-end development, interactive design, animations, and visual storytelling** to create an immersive experience inspired by one of the most iconic elements of the MCU.

---

## 💎 About the Project

The Infinity Stones are six powerful cosmic artifacts that play a central role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

This project was created as a **personal front-end development project**, with the goal of combining technical development with a visually engaging and interactive user experience.

The website presents each Infinity Stone individually, highlighting its:

- Power
- Color
- History
- Characteristics
- Role in the MCU

The interface was designed to provide an immersive experience while maintaining a responsive and intuitive navigation.

---

## 🔮 The Six Infinity Stones

| Stone | Color | Power |
|---|---|---|
| 🔵 Space Stone | Blue | Manipulation of space |
| 🟡 Mind Stone | Yellow | Manipulation of the mind |
| 🔴 Reality Stone | Red | Manipulation of reality |
| 🟣 Power Stone | Purple | Manipulation and amplification of power |
| 🟢 Time Stone | Green | Manipulation of time |
| 🟠 Soul Stone | Orange | Manipulation of souls |
